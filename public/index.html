Keyboard + mouse

  • WASD move
  • Shift run
  • Space jump
  • Mouse look, wheel to zoom
  • V first / third person

Touch

  • Left thumb: move (push far to run)
  • Right side: drag to look
  • Jump button

VR controllers

  • Left stick: move
  • Right stick: snap turn
  • A / X jump
  • Left stick click: run
  • Hold grips and swing your arms to walk
  • Trigger and grip curl the fingers
